# Trav

Short description (75 characters; Play limit 80):
Discover places, explore nearby and create personalised AI travel itineraries.

Full description:
Find inspiration for your next trip with Trav. Browse places, explore destinations and turn your travel preferences into a plan you can review and edit.

DISCOVER PLACES
Explore destinations and places with photos, source information and useful details. Search for an area and keep interesting places for later.

EXPLORE NEARBY
Choose an area or optionally use your current foreground location to discover nearby places. Location permission is optional; Trav does not use background location.

PLAN WITH AI
Describe your destination, dates, budget and travel preferences. Ask follow-up questions or build a daily itinerary, review activities and save a trip. Report concerning AI answers or plans directly inside the app.

KEEP YOUR TRIPS TOGETHER
Save places, manage travel preferences and revisit your saved plans. An email/password account is required for personalised and saved features; you can browse places as a guest.

TRAVEL WITH CONTEXT
Place information may come from open and third-party sources. AI suggestions, opening hours, prices, travel durations and availability can be inaccurate or change. Check important details with the relevant venue, transport provider or official authority before travelling. External booking links open third-party websites; Trav does not confirm reservations.
